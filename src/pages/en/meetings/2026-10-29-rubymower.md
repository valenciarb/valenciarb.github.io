Another great Valencia.rb x Hacknight collab! 🤝

**The RubyMower: installing Ruby in a stupid robot lawnmower**

More and more tech arrives in our homes: a video intercom, a robo vacuum, a
heatpump. One day you realise they are missing a feature, or that their
software is just terribly dumb, or that you just don't want it to send any
data to the cloud.

It turns out that you don't need to resign yourself to the frustrating
software it comes with. You can hack your devices!

This is an adventure of man versus cheap Leroy Merlin hardware. We will
explore electromagnetic induction, firmware hacking, hidden debug ports,
installing Ruby on microcontrollers, and much more!

See you on Thursday 29th of October at Flywire!

**Speaker** 📢 [Ignacio Huerta](https://github.com/iox)

Open source enthusiast, with a particular love of Ruby and building nice software with it. He's back with us after his February 2025 talk, [Fido, Ruby, and OpenSSL – When Payments Randomly Fail](/en/meetings/2025-02-05-fido-openssl-ruby/).
